Surface Segregation of Nanoparticles

Period / project metadata to be added

Investigated the interfacial driving forces governing spontaneous surface segregation of surface-modified nanoparticles in polymer thin films. Designed nanoparticle surface-modification strategies and quantified through-thickness particle distributions to establish relationships between segregation behavior and mechanical and optical performance.

Additional Research Projects

Stretchable Optical Films with Biomimetic Surface Patterns

Sep 2025 – Aug 2026 · NRF Master’s Research Grant

Developing transparent, stretchable adhesive films for free-form displays through elastomeric resin design and biomimetic surface patterning. Initial formulation screening evaluated adhesion, tensile behavior, stress relaxation, optical transparency, and cyclic strain recovery, while ongoing work focuses on highly elastic PUA materials and inkjet-based micropatterning.

Methods / Focus: PUA/UA resin design · Mechanical recovery · Optical transparency · Inkjet patterning
